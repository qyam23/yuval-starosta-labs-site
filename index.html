Selected Impact

Industrial proof delivered through engineering discipline.

Selected examples of process improvement, retrofit execution, and full industrial delivery in demanding operating environments.

Throughput Improvement

High-filled TiO2 compounding on 70 mm and 92 mm lines

Existing lines producing 50% to 70% TiO2 compounds were operating at low throughput. Yuval and team delivered an improvement of more than 60% through screw configuration redesign, feeding redesign, and tighter control over how minerals entered the machine.

United States Retrofit

Renewal of 50 mm and 80 mm production lines under a short shutdown window

The surrounding machine area had to be dismantled within a narrow execution period. A platform structure was prepared in advance to exact dimensions, allowing a modern feeding system to be installed rapidly and with tight control of downtime.

Relocation & Greenfield Delivery

Factory transfer in Russia and masterbatch plant execution in Poland

Rapid factory relocation in Russia and involvement in establishing a color masterbatch plant in Poland demonstrate full industrial capability, from plant definition and layout logic through installation, start-up, and production readiness.
